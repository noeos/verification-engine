# Support

Noeos supports the stable `1.x` major line for five years from its general availability. General project questions may be sent to `soporte@noeos.es`; security issues must follow `SECURITY.md`, privacy matters go to `privacidad@noeos.es`, and conduct reports go to `abuse@noeos.es`.

| Package line | Support starts                  | Security support ends |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| `1.x`        | General availability of `1.0.0` | 2031-08-31            |

Public issues must use synthetic examples and must not contain credentials, customer records, personal data, tax records, or embargoed vulnerabilities.
