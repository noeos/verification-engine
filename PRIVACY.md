# Privacy statement

Noeos Verification Engine and its CLI run locally. They do not create accounts, transmit telemetry, host payloads, retain records, or contact Noeos while processing data. Payloads, identifiers, digests, and evidence remain under the control of the integrator and the environment in which the software runs.

Identifiers, hashes, and diagnostics can constitute personal data when an integrator supplies information that identifies a person. Hashing is not automatically anonymisation. Integrators determine the lawful basis, purpose, security, retention, and data-subject response process for their own deployments.

Support and security reports must use synthetic, minimised information. Do not submit credentials, customer records, tax records, sensitive personal data, or embargoed vulnerability details in public issues. Privacy requests concerning Noeos support channels may be sent to `privacidad@noeos.es`; security reports follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

Installing packages from npm or accessing GitHub involves those providers under their respective terms and privacy notices. Those provider interactions are outside the runtime behaviour of the engine.
