# Code of conduct

## Expected conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, evaluate work against documented requirements and evidence, welcome relevant technical disagreement, protect private and security-sensitive information, and make project spaces accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, retaliation, and abuse of project access are prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project events, and private communications made in an official project role.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `abuse@noeos.es`. Do not expose a report or reporter in a public issue. Reports will be acknowledged, assessed for conflicts of interest, restricted to people needed for handling, and retained only as necessary for safety and legal obligations.

Proportionate responses may include clarification, warning, content removal, temporary restriction, permanent exclusion, or escalation to a platform or competent authority. The subject of a report will not decide their own case. Retaliation is itself a violation.

Security vulnerabilities use `SECURITY.md`; privacy requests use `privacidad@noeos.es`.

## Appeals

An affected person may request one reconsideration through `abuse@noeos.es`, identifying a factual or procedural error. A person not responsible for the original decision will review the appeal when organizational capacity permits.
